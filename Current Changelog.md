## 📢 Features and Improvements

### ✅ `Feature` Gamemode: Hardpoint
- Hold the active zone to score points for your team. The zone moves every 60 seconds.
- The announcer calls it out when a new Hardpoint comes online.

### ✅ `Feature` Death animations
- Characters now play a death animation before their body ragdolls.
- New Death Animation options in the General settings: `Animation` Plays just the death animation with no ragdoll effect (Cheaper!).

### ✅ `Feature` Take control of bots
- While spectating a bot on your team in Headquarters or Ranked, press E (X on controller) to take control of it.
- You become the bot: its name, look, class, weapons, ammo, health and scorestreaks.
- Your kills, deaths, score and medals count for the bot on the killfeed and scoreboard.
- Your own XP and challenge progress still count.
- Leaving or switching teams hands the bot back where you stood.

### 🔼 `Improvement` When spectating you can now see your spectatees health

### 🔼 `Improvement` RPG Milspec challenge now requires 25 Flyswatter medals

### 🔼 `Improvement` Shotgun Pulse challenge now requires 100 pointblank kills

### 🔼 `Improvement` You can now cook frag grenades
- If you die while cooking a grenade, it drops on the ground live.
- Your crosshair pulses once a second while cooking so you can count down the fuse.
- Mobile can cook grenades too.

## 🐛 Bugfixes
- Fixed a bug where the map intro animation would interfere with the class loadout editor UI.
- Fixed a bug where when you pressed Jump while spectating a player, it would visually make them jump.
- Fixed a bug where when you pressed the grenade key while spectating a player, it would visually make them throw a grenade.
- Fixed a bug where capturing a Headquarters would randomly stop while you were standing inside the zone.
- Fixed a bug where swapping between Anarchist and Globalist operator on the menu did not work.
- Fixed a bug where the skin previewer in the shop did not work.
- Fixed a bug where a competitive match stopped by a server shutdown counted as a loss and an abandon. The match is now cancelled and nobody wins or loses.
- Fixed a bug where the message telling you that you abandoned a competitive match often never showed. It now shows on the title screen the next time you play, even days later.
